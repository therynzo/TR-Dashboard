# TR Dashboard branding

- Brand name: **TR Dashboard**
- Copyright/creator text: **Officially made by TheRynzo**
- Default logo asset: `payload/public/tr-logo.png` (copied to Laravel's `public/tr-logo.png` by `install.sh`)
- The logo is used in the sidebar, top navigation, login/register pages, mobile authentication header, footer, favicon, and Apple touch icon.
- Admins can still override the default logo in **Admin → Settings → Brand & appearance**.
